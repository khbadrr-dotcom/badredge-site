The process

Plan, size, review. Every session.

Plan the risk

Decide what a bad day costs before the session starts. Know your loss limit, your stop and the point where you walk away.

Size to the account

Trade the size the account can survive, not the size you hope for. Add size only when the cushion has grown to carry it.

Review the evidence

Look at what you did, not what you remember. Check if your edge still looks like the one you planned with.

FAQ

Questions traders ask

What is Badr Edge?

The public side of a working futures trader's desk: the tools, guides and testing habits used to plan and review trading, shared for free. It isn't a signal service, a course or a fund.

Is it free?

Yes. The tools and guides need no account, no paywall and no trial.

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. The tools run in your browser. Files you load are read on your device, and settings are saved only in your own browser's storage.

Do you sell signals or courses?

No. There are no signals, alerts or courses here. Pine Script work is described on the Pine Script page.

Is any of this financial advice?

No. Everything here is educational. Simulations are hypothetical and rely on the inputs you give them. Your trading decisions are your own.

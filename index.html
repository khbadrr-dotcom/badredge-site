The process

Plan, size, review. Every account.

Plan the attempt

Put your real win rate, average win and average loss into the pass simulator. See how often that edge clears the target before the max loss, and how long it takes.

Size to the buffer

Start at the size the account can survive, not the size you hope for. Add contracts only as the gap between your balance and the floor grows.

Review the evidence

Import your trade list after each week. Check if the edge still looks like the one you planned with, and stress it with worse fills and smaller winners.

FAQ

Questions traders ask

Are the tools really free?

Yes. There's no account, no paywall and no trial. They're the same tools I use to plan my own accounts.

Is my trade data uploaded anywhere?

No. The simulators run entirely in your browser. CSV and Excel files are read on your device, and settings are saved only in your own browser's storage.

Which prop firms do the presets cover?

The presets approximate common rule styles (trailing end-of-day, intraday trailing, best-day consistency, buffers and caps). Firms change their rules often, so every value is editable. Check your firm's current rules before relying on any result.

Can a simulator tell me if I'll pass?

No tool can. A simulation shows how your stated edge behaves under a set of rules if the future looks like the inputs. It's a planning aid that helps you avoid sizes and rule sets that are likely to fail.

Do you sell signals, courses or strategies?

No signals and no courses. Pine Script work is described on the Pine Script page.

FAQ

Questions traders ask

What is Badr Edge?

The public side of a working futures trader's desk: the tools, indicators and testing habits used to plan and review trading, shared for free. It isn't a signal service, a course or a fund.

Is it free?

Yes. The tools need no account, no paywall and no trial. The indicators are free and open source on TradingView.

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. The tools run in your browser. Files you load are read on your device, and settings are saved only in your own browser's storage.

Do you sell signals or courses?

No. There are no signals, alerts or courses here, and no win-rate or profit promises. Private Pine Scripts are opening soon.

Is any of this financial advice?

No. Everything here is educational. Simulations are hypothetical and rely on the inputs you give them. Your trading decisions are your own.

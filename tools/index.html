Prop-firm simulators

Three Monte Carlo tools for planning evaluations, payouts and position size under trailing drawdown rules. Free, no sign-up, and everything runs in your browser.

Free

Prop Pass Simulator

Thousands of simulated evaluation attempts with your numbers and a firm's rules: trailing max loss, daily limit, best-day consistency and minimum days.

  • Pass and fail odds by contract size
  • Median days to pass
  • Why attempts fail
  • TradingView CSV import
Open tool →
Free

Payout Simulator

Models the funded account after you pass: winning-day rules, buffers, splits and caps, so you know how long a first payout takes.

  • Odds of reaching a first payout
  • Days to payout and to the cap
  • Compare firm-style rule sets
  • Portfolio of strategies
Open tool →
Free

Contract Sizing

Finds the contract size that keeps a trailing max loss safe, scales it as your buffer grows, and stress-tests the ladder eight ways.

  • Size for today's buffer
  • Short-term forecast fan
  • Stress and out-of-sample tests
  • CSV and Excel import
Open tool →

Tip: export your strategy's "List of trades" from the TradingView Strategy Tester and load the CSV. Real trades with real dates keep losing streaks and bad weeks intact, which is where most accounts fail.

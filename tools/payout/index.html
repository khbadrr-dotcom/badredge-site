Prop Payout Simulator

Simulates the funded account after you pass: no profit target, the same max loss, and each firm's payout rules. It shows how likely you are to reach your first payout and the payout cap, how long each takes, and at what size.

Running the first simulation…

Each attempt starts a fresh funded account and replays random trading sessions drawn from your strategy's results. It counts the first day every payout rule is met, then keeps trading without withdrawing until you can take the full cap, lose the account, or run out of sessions. Drawdown is checked on closed trades only. Past results do not guarantee future results. This is a planning tool, not financial advice.

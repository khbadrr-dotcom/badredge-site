Tested · no edge

Opening range breakout: Pro Max

Mark the high and low of a fixed 30-minute window. Trade the first close beyond that range, or the first fair-value gap that forms after it, with the stop on the other side of the range.

Markets: MGC, MES, MYM, MNQ, SIL, PL
Scope: 5m and 15m charts · tuned on Oct 2025 to Oct 2026 · replayed on Oct 2021 to Oct 2025

What was tested

  • Hundreds of thousands of setting combinations per market (window start, entry type, stop, target, filters), searched on the most recent 12 months.
  • The best setting per market was frozen and replayed unchanged on four earlier years it had never seen.
  • One market (MYM) also got a pre-registered frozen test, written down before it was run.
  • 15 trader-designed filters (trend, volatility, session, day-of-week and more) were added to the best gold version, each compared against a random placebo filter.

The in-sample column is the best result out of a very large search on the same data, so it is expected to look good. The added trader filters: 0 of 15 beat a random placebo filter, and all 15 made the base version worse.

All dollar figures are hypothetical backtest results for one contract, after exchange and broker fees and one tick of slippage on each side of every market or stop order. They describe the past under stated assumptions and do not predict future results.

Verdict

Tested · no edge

No edge beyond selection. The picked settings sit inside what a random-direction search produces, and both pre-registered frozen tests failed.

Lesson

A 70 to 80% win rate on the year you optimised on is the normal output of a big search, not a sign of an edge. Random-direction picks from the same pipeline also showed 77 to 92% win rates.

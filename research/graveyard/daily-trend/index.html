Tested · no edge

Daily trend signals traded intraday

Use a classic daily trend signal (moving-average crossover or 12-month momentum) to choose the direction, then hold only inside the trading day.

Markets: 10 markets
Scope: EWMAC and TSMOM signals · entries at 01:00, 10:00 or 16:30 GMT+3 · frozen test 2022 to 2025

What was tested

  • Four literature-fixed variants, about 8,850 trades each.
  • Pre-registered frozen test with Holm correction and power calculated in advance.

What happened

  • All four were negative after costs: −0.031R to −0.043R per trade.
  • Gross results before costs were also slightly negative.
  • Power was 0.77 to 1.00, so an edge of a useful size would likely have been detected.

All dollar figures are hypothetical backtest results for one contract, after exchange and broker fees and one tick of slippage on each side of every market or stop order. They describe the past under stated assumptions and do not predict future results.

Verdict

Tested · no edge

No edge.

Lesson

Trend following earns over weeks and months. Cutting it into single sessions removes most of what it captures.

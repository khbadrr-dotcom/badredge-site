Parabolic SAR with 200 EMA

Take Parabolic SAR flips only in the direction of the 200 EMA, with volume above average.

Markets: MGC, SIL, PL, MHG, M2K
Scope: 5m to 60m · volume and delta filters · frozen test on MGC 30m

What was tested

  • Timeframe scan from 5 to 60 minutes and filter tuning on the last 12 months.
  • Frozen replay of the gold 30m version on 2021 to 2025.

What happened

  • Best 12-month picks sat inside the random-direction null (MGC 0.885, SIL 0.495 percentile; 0.95 needed).
  • Walk-forward second half was negative or broke the $2,000 drawdown limit in 9 of 15 cases.
  • MGC 30m frozen replay: profit factor 0.75, −$8,012.

All dollar figures are hypothetical backtest results for one contract, after exchange and broker fees and one tick of slippage on each side of every market or stop order. They describe the past under stated assumptions and do not predict future results.

Verdict

Tested · no edge

No edge.

Lesson

Classic indicator combinations are already in every chart package. Expect them to be priced in, and test them like anything else.

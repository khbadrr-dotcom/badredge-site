Tested · no edge

14-strategy cross-market portfolio

Individually weak strategies on different markets, combined with risk managed at portfolio level, might add up to something steady.

Markets: MGC, MES, MYM, MNQ, SIL, PL, M2K, MCL, MHG, M6E
Scope: The 14 best opening range and Initial Balance picks, run together · 1 micro each

What was tested

  • The 14 frozen picks combined, settings never changed.
  • Replay on 2021 to 2025, data none of them was chosen on.
  • Also: a 'best 5 of 14' subset and a cross-market gap fade held for hours.

What happened

Last 12 months (where picks were chosen)2021 to 2025 (unseen)
Net, after costs+$89,458−$31,794
Max drawdown$1,549$34,196
Win rate75.5%below 75% on most picks
Picks making money14 of 141 of 14 (+$77)
Years making money0 of 5

The left column is the best of many thousands of combinations on the same year, so it is in-sample by construction. The 'best 5' subset lost −$9,641. The cross-market gap fade lost on all 9 versions.

All dollar figures are hypothetical backtest results for one contract, after exchange and broker fees and one tick of slippage on each side of every market or stop order. They describe the past under stated assumptions and do not predict future results.

Verdict

Tested · no edge

No edge. Combining picks diversified the risk but could not create an edge the single picks did not have.

Lesson

Fourteen small losers add up to one large loser. Diversification reduces noise; it cannot turn a zero edge into a positive one.

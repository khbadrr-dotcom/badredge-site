Asian range breakout (gold)

Mark the range of the Asian session. Trade the breakout during London and New York hours, stop at the other side of the range, target 1R, time exit in the afternoon.

Markets: MGC
Scope: 1h chart · rules written before testing · split 2025 / 2026

What was tested

  • One rule set written down before any test (no grid search).
  • In-sample year 2025, then an out-of-sample test on 2026 to October.

Verdict

Exploratory

Exploratory (on hold). It passed its own out-of-sample bar, but a reviewer asked for gold data back to 2015 before anything can be concluded, and that test has not been run. The drawdown is also above the $2,000 limit for one contract.

Lesson

One good out-of-sample year is a reason to test further, not a reason to trade. A reviewer asked for about ten years of gold history before going further.

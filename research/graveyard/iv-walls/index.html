Tested · no edge

Options open-interest walls

The strikes with the largest open interest above and below price act as walls: price rejects them or breaks through and retests.

Markets: MES, MNQ
Scope: Quarterly and serial ES/NQ options · 5m and 15m · 72 settings per model

What was tested

  • Three models (wick rejection, breakout, break and retest).
  • Every setting run on the real walls and on placebo strikes the same distance away.
  • Open interest used only as it was known at the time (2 to 5 days old).

What happened

  • All six market and model cells lost after costs: −0.03R to −0.27R per trade.
  • The best wall settings were no better than the best placebo settings.

All dollar figures are hypothetical backtest results for one contract, after exchange and broker fees and one tick of slippage on each side of every market or stop order. They describe the past under stated assumptions and do not predict future results.

Verdict

Tested · no edge

No edge, in this scope. Weekly and 0DTE options were not tested.

Lesson

Placebo levels are the fair test for any level-based idea. If made-up levels do as well, the real ones carry no information.

Tested · no edge

Opening range: Break and Hold

Five levels inside each opening range (high, 75%, middle, 25%, low). A 15-minute candle that closes beyond a level is a 'hold', and the trade goes in that direction with an EMA trend filter.

Markets: MGC, MES, MYM, MNQ
Scope: 15m signals · two windows per day · same tune-then-replay design

What was tested

  • Full grid of windows, levels, filters and exits on four markets, ranked on the last 12 months.
  • Walk-forward check: choose the best setting on the first half of the year, then trade it on the second half.
  • The best MYM setting was frozen and replayed on 2021 to 2025.

What happened

CheckResult
MGC walk-forwardfirst half, picked there: +$10,889 · second half, unseen: −$955
MYM #1, in-sample 12m245 trades · 71% WR · +$2,660
MYM #1, 2021 to 2025 unseen1,064 trades · 64% WR · −$1,812 · DD $2,844

All dollar figures are hypothetical backtest results for one contract, after exchange and broker fees and one tick of slippage on each side of every market or stop order. They describe the past under stated assumptions and do not predict future results.

Verdict

Tested · no edge

No edge beyond selection on any of the four markets.

Lesson

A walk-forward collapse (large profit on the half you chose from, a loss on the next half) is the fastest way to spot an over-fitted setting.

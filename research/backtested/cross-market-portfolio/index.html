14-strategy cross-market portfolio

Individually weak strategies on different markets, combined with risk managed at portfolio level, might add up to something steady.

Markets: MGC, MES, MYM, MNQ, SIL, PL, M2K, MCL, MHG, M6E
Scope: The 14 best opening range and Initial Balance picks, run together · 1 micro each

What was tested

  • The 14 frozen picks combined, settings never changed.
  • Replay on 2021 to 2025, data none of them was chosen on.
  • Also: a 'best 5 of 14' subset and a cross-market gap fade held for hours.

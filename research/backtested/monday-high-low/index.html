Monday high and low

Monday's high and low act as weekly reference levels. Trade the reaction at the next session open, or a sweep and reclaim during the week.

Markets: MNQ, MES, MYM
Scope: 9,120 settings · 12m search · frozen test 2021 to 2025

What was tested

  • 9,120 settings on the last 12 months.
  • Pre-registered frozen test of the best four on 2021 to 2025.

What happened

  • 12-month picks beat the selection null only weakly, and walk-forward failed on all three markets.
  • Frozen: 4 of 4 no edge. MNQ primary −0.056R per trade.

All dollar figures are hypothetical backtest results for one contract, after exchange and broker fees and one tick of slippage on each side of every market or stop order. They describe the past under stated assumptions and do not predict future results.

Verdict

Tested · no edge

No edge, in this scope.

Lesson

A weak pass on correlated markets (three US indices) counts as one result, not three.

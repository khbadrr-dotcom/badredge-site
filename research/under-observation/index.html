Noise-area momentum on MNQ

A published academic rule, tested exactly as written on MNQ in New York hours. It is research, it is not validated, and it is now being logged on new data with no changes.

The rule

This is the published rule from Zarattini, Aziz and Barbon (2024), tested exactly as written with nothing tuned:

  • For each minute of the New York session, measure the average absolute move from the open over the previous 14 sessions. That builds a 'noise area' band around the open.
  • Every half hour from 10:00 to 15:30 New York time, be long if price is above the band and above session VWAP, short if below both, and flat otherwise.
  • Flat by 22:00 GMT+3 at the latest. One micro contract, no leverage scaling.

The frozen test

Rules were written and hashed before the run. It was run once on July 2023 to October 2026, a window never used for this rule.

Status: in forward testing. This is research, not a recommendation and not a signal. No result here is a promise.

What happens next

The frozen rule is logged on new data from 22 October 2026, with no changes. A handful of weeks proves nothing either way: at roughly 180 trades a year, one year of new data alone cannot settle it. The plan is to pool new data with the frozen window and re-judge it under the same pre-registered rules.

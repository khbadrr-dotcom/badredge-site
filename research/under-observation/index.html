Under observation

Noise-area momentum on MNQ

The closest thing to a lead this desk has found. It is not validated, it fails the desk's own risk limits, and it is published here only as a research note being watched on new data.

Formal verdict: INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE · Market: MNQ, New York hours · Updated 12 November 2026

The rule

This is the published rule from Zarattini, Aziz and Barbon (2024), tested exactly as written with nothing tuned:

  • For each minute of the New York session, measure the average absolute move from the open over the previous 14 sessions. That builds a 'noise area' band around the open.
  • Every half hour from 10:00 to 15:30 New York time, be long if price is above the band and above session VWAP, short if below both, and flat otherwise.
  • Flat by 22:00 GMT+3 at the latest. One micro contract, no leverage scaling.

The frozen test

Rules were written and hashed before the run. It was run once on July 2023 to October 2026, a window never used for this rule.

Why it is not validated

  • Missed the Holm bar. p = 0.0135 against a required 0.0125. Close is not a pass.
  • Concentrated. The top 10 days carried 98% of the net profit. The limit is 50%.
  • Drawdown too large. $2,615 for one micro, above the $2,000 limit.
  • Win rate 42%, far below the 75% acceptance bar.
  • Market-specific. The same rule lost on MES, MYM, M2K and seven other markets, and on Tokyo and London sessions.
  • Season-dependent. About +$23 per trade in summer and +$2 in winter, because the fixed GMT+3 exit moves against New York time.

Status: under observation. Even if later data confirmed the effect, it would be 'real but currently unsuitable' under the desk's risk limits. It is not a recommendation and not a signal.

What happens next

The frozen rule is logged on new data from 22 October 2026, with no changes. A handful of weeks proves nothing either way: at roughly 180 trades a year, one year of new data alone cannot settle it. The plan is to pool new data with the frozen window and re-judge it under the same pre-registered rules.

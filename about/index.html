Who is behind Badr Edge.

How Badr trades

The work pairs real-market execution with systematic strategy building, quantitative testing and strict risk control. Most of the trading runs through prop firms: passing evaluations, trading funded accounts under each firm's drawdown rules and moving up to live funded accounts at four firms, with selection for a live-capital trading program. Alongside the prop accounts, Badr trades personal CFD brokerage accounts.

Execution is a discretionary candlestick price-action process, built over two years and traded mostly in gold and US index futures across the Asia, London and New York sessions.

Why it exists

The prop-firm world is loud: screenshots of wins, promises of fast funding, strategies sold on perfect backtests. Little of it answers the questions that decide whether an account survives. How big is my real cushion today? How many losses in a row can this size take? Does my edge still look like the one I planned with?

Every tool here started as something built for real accounts. The research is published in the open, and all of it requires forward testing.

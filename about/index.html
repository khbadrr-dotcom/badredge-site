About

Badr means full moon.

In Arabic, badr is the moon at its fullest: the night you can see the whole picture. That's the idea behind this site. See the full risk picture before you place the trade.

Who's behind it

I'm a futures trader. I trade gold and US index futures, mostly micro contracts, through prop-firm accounts, using a discretionary price-action process I've refined over the past two years. Alongside that I build and test systematic strategies in Pine Script.

Every tool on this site started as something I built for my own accounts: to decide how many contracts to trade, how long an evaluation is likely to take, and when a payout is realistic. They were useful enough that I decided to make them public and free.

Why it exists

The prop-firm world is loud. Most of what traders see is screenshots of wins, promises of fast funding and strategies sold with perfect backtests. Very little of it helps you answer the boring questions that actually decide whether an account survives:

  • How big is my real cushion today?
  • How many losses in a row can this size survive?
  • Does my edge still look like the edge I planned with?

Badr Edge exists to answer those questions with tools and evidence, not hype.

What you can expect

  • Free tools that run in your browser and keep your data on your device.
  • Plain-English guides with worked numbers.
  • Honesty about limits. Simulations depend on their inputs, and past results don't predict future ones.

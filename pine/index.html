How a strategy gets tested

Most backtests are too kind. These aren't.

  • Frozen rules. Settings are fixed before the test, so the result can't be tuned after the fact.
  • Real costs. Commission per contract and at least one tick of slippage on every trade.
  • Unseen data. Sized and tuned on earlier months, then judged on months it never saw.
  • Stress tests. Doubled slippage, losses 50% bigger, winners 10% smaller.
  • Many-tests check. When many variants are tried, results are corrected so a lucky one doesn't look like an edge.
  • Independent replica. The Pine logic is rebuilt outside TradingView and compared trade by trade.
